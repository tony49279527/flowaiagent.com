### 1.2 产品使用场景(典型应用情境/流程)



#### **场景分类(从大到小排序)**



**1. 全车漆面修复与美容(最常见)**

* **触发条件**: 新车dealer swirls、旧车氧化层、日常洗车划痕

* **典型流程**:&#x20;

  * 洗车→黏土处理→粗抛(V-series compound)→精抛(polish)→上蜡/镀晶

* **证据**: "polished and waxed my crew cab truck with just one battery""entire truck in a few hours"

**2. 局部缺陷处理**

* **应用点**: Spider webbing、swirl marks、水印、鸟粪蚀刻

* **证据**: "completely removes light swirl marks""remove bird dropping etchings"

**3. 大面积氧化层去除**

* **对象**: 长期户外停放车辆、船舶、房车

* **证据**: "took my chalky white boat into a shiny new boat""sitting outside since 2013"

**4. 透明涂层维护**

* **目标**: 大灯翻新、镜面抛光、clear coat恢复

* **证据**: "headlights restoration""polishing concrete countertops"

**5. 细节区域处理**

* **区域**: B柱、车标周围、格栅边缘、门把手凹槽

* **工具需求**: 3英寸小抛光机(如主产品未涵盖)

**6. 打蜡/封釉应用**

* **频率**: 季度维护或镀晶前准备

* **证据**: "makes waxing a breeze""ceramic coating preparation"

**7. 非汽车应用**

* **拓展场景**: 家具木材、大理石台面、金属表面、混凝土抛光

* **证据**: "use on furniture, floors, boats, marble, wood, and metal"

* **根源**:初学者不了解DA polisher的pad rotation维护重要性

* **触发场景**:在edges/curves施压不当,造成burn-through

* **用户特征**:首次使用者,未观看教学视频

**原因2:抛光垫快速磨损**

* **技术原因**:配套pads可能采用标准密度泡沫,非premium材质

* **使用模式**:Heavy compounding作业,单个pad过度使用

* **成本敏感度**:用户期望pad寿命更长以降低总拥有成本

**Section 2: Quick Start Guide / 快速入门(3步骤)**



**Step 1: Preparation / 准备工作**

**Step 3: Polishing Technique / 抛光技巧**

* Apply 4-5 pea-sized dots of product on pad / 在抛光垫上点4-5个豌豆大小的产品

* Work in 2ft x 2ft sections / 分区作业(2英尺x2英尺)

### 5.4 Q\&A板块



#### **Frequently Asked Questions / 常见问题**



***



**Q1: Is this suitable for complete beginners? / 完全新手能用吗?**



**A (EN)**: Yes! The dual-action (DA) design is the safest type for beginners. Unlike rotary polishers that can burn through paint, the TORQX's random orbital motion disperses heat and prevents damage. Start with the finishing pad on speed 3-4 to build confidence.



**A (中文)**: 可以!双轨道(DA)设计是新手最安全的选择。不同于旋转抛光机易烧穿漆面,TORQX的随机轨道运动分散热量防止损伤。建议从finishing垫+3-4档速度开始练习建立信心。



***



**Q2: Can I use this on ceramic coated cars? / 能用于已镀晶的车吗?**



**A (EN)**: Not recommended. Polishing removes a thin layer of clear coat, which would remove the ceramic coating. Only use this BEFORE applying ceramic coating for paint correction, or if you plan to re-coat after polishing.



**A (中文)**: 不建议。抛光会去除薄层clear coat,同时会去除镀晶层。仅在镀晶\*\*前\*\*用于漆面修复,或抛光后准备重新镀晶时使用。



***



**Q3: How often should I polish my car? / 多久抛光一次?**



**A (EN)**: Most cars only need polishing 1-2 times per year, or when visible defects appear. Over-polishing thins the clear coat. For maintenance, use wax/sealant every 3 months instead.



**A (中文)**: 大多数车每年仅需抛光1-2次,或出现明显瑕疵时。过度抛光会减薄clear coat。日常维护建议每3个月打蜡/封釉即可。



***



**Q4: What's the difference between compounds and polishes? / 研磨剂和抛光剂有何区别?**



**A (EN)**:

* **Compound** (V36): More abrasive, removes deeper scratches (like 2000-grit sandpaper). Use with orange cutting pad.

* **Polish** (V38): Finer abrasive, removes haze and refines finish (like 3000-grit). Use with white/black pad.

* Always finish with polish for best gloss.

**A (中文)**:

* **研磨剂**(V36):研磨力强,去除深划痕(相当于2000砂纸)。搭配橙色粗抛垫。

* **抛光剂**(V38):细腻研磨,去除雾化并精修(相当于3000砂纸)。搭配白色/黑色垫。

* 务必用抛光剂收尾以达最佳光泽。

***



**Q5: Can I use aftermarket pads from other brands? / 能用其他品牌的抛光垫吗?**



**A (EN)**: Yes, as long as they have 5-inch hook-and-loop (Velcro) backing compatible with the TORQX backing plate. Popular brands like Lake Country, Meguiar's, and Hex-Logic work well.



**A (中文)**: 可以,只要是5英寸魔术贴式且兼容TORQX背板即可。Lake Country、Meguiar's、Hex-Logic等品牌均适配。



***



**Q6: Why is my paint hazy after polishing? / 为什么抛光后漆面发雾?**



**A (EN)**: Common causes:

* Used too aggressive pad/compound without finishing step

* Polished too fast (arm speed >2 inches/sec)

* Pad was dirty/wet

**Solution**: Re-polish with white/black pad + V38 polish at slower speed.



**A (中文)**: 常见原因:

* 使用过粗糙的垫/研磨剂后未做精抛步骤

* 移动过快(臂速>每秒2英寸)

* 垫片脏污/潮湿

**解决**:用白色/黑色垫+V38抛光剂慢速重新抛光。



***



**Q7: How do I know when to replace pads? / 何时该换新抛光垫?**



**A (EN)**: Replace when:

* Foam becomes compressed and loses thickness

* Velcro backing separates from foam

* Excessive tearing or chunks missing

* Pad no longer holds products well

Average lifespan: 5-10 full vehicle details per pad.



**A (中文)**: 以下情况需更换:

* 泡沫压缩变薄失去弹性

* 魔术贴与泡沫分离

* 撕裂严重或缺块

* 无法良好吸附产品

平均寿命:每片可完成5-10次全车美容。



***



**Q8: Can this remove deep scratches? / 能去除深划痕吗?**



**A (EN)**: It can remove scratches **in the clear coat** (up to \~2000-grit level). Deep scratches that reach base coat/primer cannot be fully removed and may require professional wet sanding or touch-up paint.



**A (中文)**: 可去除\*\*clear coat内\*\*的划痕(约2000砂纸级)。深至底漆/腻子层的划痕无法完全去除,需专业湿磨或补漆处理。



***



**Q9: Is it safe to use on matte/satin finishes? / 能用于哑光/缎面漆吗?**



**A (EN)**: ❌ **NO!** This polisher creates gloss and will ruin matte finishes. Matte paint requires special non-abrasive cleaners only.



**A (中文)**: ❌ **不行!** 抛光机会产生光泽破坏哑光效果。哑光漆仅能使用专用非研磨清洁剂。



***



**Q10: What should I do if I burn through the paint? / 如果磨穿漆面怎么办?**



**A (EN)**: Unfortunately, burn-through (exposing primer/metal) cannot be fixed by polishing. Options:

* Touch-up paint pen for small spots

* Professional respray for larger areas

**Prevention**: Avoid edges, use light pressure, keep pad flat.



**A (中文)**: 不幸的是,磨穿(露出底漆/金属)无法通过抛光修复。选项:

* 小面积用补漆笔

* 大面积需专业重新喷漆

**预防**:避开棱角、轻压、保持垫片水平。
